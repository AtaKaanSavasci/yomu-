## Dictionary checks and attribution

All **85 new word/kanji records** match written forms and explicit readings in [EDRDG JMdict](https://www.edrdg.org/jmdict/j_jmdict.html), English snapshot marked `JMdict created: 2026-11-07`. Matching respects `re_restr`; dictionary senses were also reviewed for the selected study meanings. Homographs such as ライム, 解凍 and 再帰 use their fruit/computing sense in the example. Meanings are short, contextual study glosses rather than exhaustive dictionary definitions. Example-sentence readings and translations were authored for Yomu; pair matching is not independent approval of every example.

The existing preparation snapshot was reused; it is not presented as the latest dictionary.
