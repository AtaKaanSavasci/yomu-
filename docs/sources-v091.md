# Vocabulary expansion · 0.9.1

100 original study entries were added in `dist/src/vocabulary-november.js`. Every entry has Japanese writing, an explicit kana reading, a Turkish and English study gloss, an original Japanese example with kana, and both example translations. No Genki vocabulary list or textbook exercise was copied.

Each N5–N1 band now contains 60 words. These remain approximate editorial study bands, including specialist fruit/agriculture and software terms. They are not official or complete JLPT vocabulary lists. Dictionary matching does not establish a word's JLPT level or human approval of the original examples/translations.

All 100 written-form/reading pairs were matched to entries in [EDRDG JMdict](https://www.edrdg.org/jmdict/j_jmdict.html), English edition snapshot marked `JMdict created: 2026-11-07`. Reading restrictions (`re_restr`) were respected; mixed kana and equivalent hiragana readings were normalised for matching. In particular, 歯ブラシ / はブラシ and 延長コード / えんちょうコード are explicit content readings, not kanji-derived guesses. The new short study glosses and examples were authored for Yomu rather than copied dictionary paragraphs. The dictionary was consulted during preparation; no dictionary download or API request is added to the learning flow.

Existing stored answer events and review identities are not rewritten. The shared word pool makes the additions available in word practice, saved reviews, listening quizzes and subsequent level-practice tests; active sessions keep their existing snapshots. Sentence/grammar banks remain 120/50.
